About Me

I am a PhD student at the School of Creative Media, City University of Hong Kong. My research lies at the intersection of game studies, digital media, and feminist media studies. I examine how digital games create emotional, affective, and intimate experiences for players.

Before starting my PhD, I completed my Master of Arts in Global Communication at The Chinese University of Hong Kong and my Bachelor of Arts in Japanese Studies at Renmin University of China.

Research Interests

Virtual Intimacy and Romance in Video Games

Exploring how otome games and interactive narratives construct virtual romantic relationships and emotional connections between players and fictional characters.

Player Affect and Gaming Communities

Investigating affective experiences, online gaming communities, fan cultures, and collective identities formed through digital games.

Gacha Mechanics and Game Monetization

Examining loot boxes, probability disclosure, gacha systems, and regulatory issues in Japanese mobile games.
